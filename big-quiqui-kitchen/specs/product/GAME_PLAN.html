No title in the sample is mainly a kitchen game. Summer Camp is the only Builder title with a serve scene and slicing scenes. Smolsies Cute Pet House has the cooking code.

Take

  • Shell and scene flow from Summer Camp. Cooker logic from Smolsies (BaseCooker, CookParams). Food type, tier and reaction as data from Giggle Babies (KitchenFoodsData). Ingredient give and receive from Dopples.

Skip

  • Customers who leave if you are slow, and anything with a timer.

Also look at

  • Dopples World has the biggest cooking vocabulary (74 related types: Cook, Recipe, Burger, Glaze, Bakery).
