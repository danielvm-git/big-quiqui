  • Content to make
    • 10 pets with 3 looks each, and 4 rooms.
    • About 12 toys and the furniture for the rooms.
    • Egg, hatch and pet sounds, and a voice line per room.
  • ProgressionHatching and caring fill the collection book. Stars open new rooms.
  • Risks
    • QuiQuiRig is built for one hero. Many pets need a lighter rig.
    • Fluvsies depends on live events and server time. This game must work fully offline.
    • Per-pet needs can turn into pressure. They only change an icon.
    • The hatch template is new and needs its own scope.
  • First playable sliceOne room, three pets, one toy station and one egg.Eighth game. It shares the TutoTOONS type "pet care and collect" with Care, but it is Form C: many pets in rooms instead of one hero.
