What is specific to this game

Game-specific moduleWhat it doesEvidence
Beat clockOne clock driven by the music. Everything else syncs to it.DancingInputManager, DanceGameController
Beat mapsEach song is a file: tempo and a list of beats.DancingLevelData
FormationsFriends move into shapes as the song goes on.DancingFormation

  • Content to make
    • 3 songs of about 60 seconds, original or family-made.
    • 4 dancing friends and a stage.
    • Music rights must be clear. No licensed tracks.
  • ProgressionEach song gives stars. Stars unlock outfits and new songs.
  • Risks
    • Not in the Builder at all, so it needs a custom engine.
    • Audio timing on iPad must be measured, not assumed.
    • Needs original music.
  • First playable sliceOne song, one friend, tap on the beat.Seventh game. It grows the existing duck dance (t_rhythm_pose) into a full rhythm template with audio timing.
